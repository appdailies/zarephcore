Help center

Answers to the questions we hear most often.

Getting started

Download Dailies from the App Store or Google Play, create an account with your phone number or email, and you're ready to post your first story.

Account & profile

  • Change your username or profile photo from Settings → Profile.
  • Switch between a public and private account at any time.
  • Deactivate or permanently delete your account from Settings → Account.

Stories

  • Stories on Dailies disappear after 24 hours by default.
  • Use Close Friends to share with a smaller, hand-picked audience.
  • You can save a story to your device before it expires.

Troubleshooting

If the app is crashing or a story won't upload, try updating to the latest version first. If the problem continues, reach out to us and we'll help you sort it out.

Still stuck?

Contact our support team and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.
