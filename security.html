Security

How we protect your information.

Our approach

We build security into our infrastructure from the start — including encryption in transit, strict access controls, and regular internal reviews of how data is stored and handled.

Data protection

  • All traffic between the Dailies app and our servers is encrypted in transit.
  • Access to user data internally is limited and logged.
  • We run regular security reviews as our systems grow.

Report a vulnerability

If you believe you've found a security issue with any Zareph Core product, please let us know through our contact page with as much detail as possible. We take all reports seriously and will follow up promptly.

Responsible disclosure

We ask that you give us a reasonable amount of time to investigate and address any issue before making it public, and that you avoid accessing or modifying data that isn't yours during testing.
