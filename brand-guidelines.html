Brand guidelines

How to use the Zareph Core name, logo, and colors correctly.

Our logo

The Zareph Core mark is three overlapping spheres forming a central core. Always use the approved logo files — don't recolor, distort, or recreate the mark.

Color palette

Our primary palette moves from deep teal to bright green, symbolizing growth from a strong core.

#085041
#1D9E75
#97C459
#FFFFFF

Typography

We use a clean system sans-serif across all Zareph Core products and marketing — prioritizing legibility over ornamentation.

Using our name

  • Refer to us as "Zareph Core" on first mention, "Zareph" is acceptable after that.
  • Product names (Dailies) should be capitalized and not abbreviated.
  • Don't imply partnership or endorsement without written permission.

Requesting assets

For press or partner use of our logo and brand assets, please reach out via our contact page.
