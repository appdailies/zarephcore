Safety center

Our approach to keeping people safe on Zareph Core products.

Our commitment

Safety is core to everything we build. We invest in tools, policies, and a dedicated Trust & Safety team to help protect our community, especially younger users.

Tools you control

  • Block or restrict any account without them being notified.
  • Report a story, message, or account directly from the app.
  • Control who can reply to your stories or send you messages.

Community guidelines

We don't allow content that promotes violence, harassment, hate speech, or exploitation of any kind. Accounts that violate our guidelines may be restricted or removed.

Supporting younger users

We build extra protections for teen accounts, including default private accounts and limits on who can contact them.

Report a concern

If you or someone you know is at risk, please contact local emergency services first. For platform-specific concerns, reach out through our contact page.
